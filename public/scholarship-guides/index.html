Build a scholarship search from more than one source

Use search results to discover possibilities, then verify each opportunity with the official sponsor or provider before relying on its details or applying.

Start with your high-school counselor or college financial-aid office. Add community foundations, civic organizations, employers, state higher-education agencies, and organizations connected to your intended major or interests. Federal Student Aid recommends combining school, local, state, employer, and interest-related sources rather than relying on one directory.

For every opportunity, record the sponsor's official application page, deadline, award conditions, eligibility rules, and required documents. A listing in a search engine or directory is only a lead; it does not prove that the award is open or that you qualify.

Protect personal information. Avoid putting passwords, Social Security numbers, essays, or detailed financial records into public search queries or untrusted forms. Keep private student details on services you have independently checked and trust.
